### Engineering Focus

- **Electronic Hardware Architecture & Schematic Design:** Circuit topology, component selection, and power management.
- **Multi-layer PCB Layout:** DRC rules, ground copper pours, high-frequency antenna clearance, and manufacturing documentation.
- **Microcontrollers & Embedded Platforms:** ESP32 architecture, sensor integration, and hardware peripheral interfacing.
- **Power & Battery Management:** Charging circuits, voltage regulation, decoupling, and protection stages.
- **Design for Manufacturing (DFM):** Gerber generation, pick-and-place files, and component lifecycle verification (LCSC / Octopart).
